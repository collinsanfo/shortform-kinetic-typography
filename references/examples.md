# Reusable requests

## Reference-led restyle

> Use $shortform-kinetic-typography to restyle this video's words using my references. Keep the narration, wording, product artwork and duration unchanged.

Compare references with the existing video, identify the visible gap, and select related treatments. The proof should change actual hierarchy and integration; a faster camera over identical headings is insufficient.

## Calm explainer

> Give this explainer elegant text emphasis. Keep it calm and precise, with no bouncing words.

Use restrained scale contrast and continuity. Let short complete phrases settle. “Kinetic” does not require constant activity or every recipe.

## Text behind a speaker

> Put the phrase behind this speaker while they move, keeping the face clear.

Establish whether the source is a still, static-camera video or moving-camera footage. Use an appropriate matte and tracking; inspect the whole affected interval. A still cutout does not prove the moving effect.

## Floor text

> Make this last line feel painted on the ground in a walking shot.

Derive and track the plane, apply foreground occlusion, and match scene contrast/blur. Keep the essential words at a readable angle. A static perspective preview establishes placement, not finished tracking.

## Graphic canvas

> Make an animated canvas for these five phrases. No presenter or voice generation is needed.

Use type hierarchy as the storytelling device and connect thoughts through direction, scale or a shared word. Do not add an unsolicited avatar or unrelated stock footage. The example plan is a starting point, not a mandatory template.

## Review only

Answer from observable hierarchy, spacing, weight, colour and layering. Identify concrete changes without silently launching full production or claiming unseen motion/performance.
