# QA and delivery

Choose checks proportional to the deliverable. A still design does not need a video audit; a finished film needs the actual encoded file checked.

## Visual checks

- Dominant word is meaningful; reading order and complete phrase are clear.
- Support remains readable at phone size; the intended fonts and weights load.
- Stems, accents, outlines and descenders are intact, including italic overhangs.
- Line breaks preserve grammar and avoid isolated unhelpful words.
- Colour/weight emphasis is selective rather than universal.
- Faces and important product details stay clear.
- Local contrast works through the affected shot, not only on a flat colour swatch.

Inspect transformed bounds after rotation, camera and perspective. A valid original rectangle does not prove the final word fits. Thin outlines may disappear under compression; heavy glow may blur the letters.

## Depth and motion

Check matte edges, intentional occlusion and word recognition. Surface type should stay attached to its plane without sliding. Physical interaction needs convincing contact, movement and occlusion. A static proof does not establish tracking or motion quality.

Inspect short exposures and transitions as well as settled frames. Compare readable time to phrase length. Check first/final frames and the relationship between the spoken thought and the type, including documented editorial leads.

For frozen wording, compare source tokens with the non-decorative reading order. Also inspect the film for spelling and glyph substitutions. Token equality does not prove viewers will follow the visual reading order.

## Technical checks

Verify dimensions, aspect ratio, duration, frame count and successful decode when relevant. Compare canonical audio through a suitable lossless path when preservation matters. Check the final spoken word and intentional closing hold. Use frozen-asset hashes when the brief requires exact preservation of originals.

Technical execution and advertising effectiveness are separate. Do not claim a hook converts well because its styling resembles popular examples or their screenshots show high view counts.

## Portable package

Include the necessary plan/project, assets and font licences. Exclude unused caches, rejected exports, private recordings, customer data, secrets and unlicensed reference media. Prefer relative paths. Reopen in a separate extraction directory and load the project or render a representative frame; opening the ZIP alone is not enough.

Record the artifact/hash, technical properties, performed checks and limits. Distinguish technical validation, sampled visual inspection, continuous viewing/listening, owner creative approval, and local preparation versus public publication. Report only what was established. Show the usable result in a concise final handoff.
